About this dashboard. This page monitors the electricity supply environment for Kaimbwala village (U.T. Chandigarh) and the adjoining Naya Gaon area that shares PIN 160103. Weather, solar irradiance, air-quality and postal data are genuinely live, pulled directly from public APIs for these exact coordinates and refreshed continuously.

The electrical quantities — voltage, frequency, feeder load, current, transformer status — are produced by a physics-based model calibrated to Indian distribution practice (230 V/50 Hz, 5 MW 11 kV rural feeder, IEGC frequency band, IS 12360 voltage limits). The model is coupled to the live weather feed: real temperature drives cooling load, real irradiance offsets demand with rooftop solar, and real wind gusts, rainfall and thunderstorm codes raise the fault-probability that can trip the feeder. This is the closest achievable approximation because no DISCOM in India currently publishes an open, real-time public SCADA feed at individual-feeder granularity.

Not an official source. For authoritative supply status, planned shutdowns, billing or complaints, contact CPDL (Chandigarh) or PSPCL (Naya Gaon/Mohali) on 1912. In an emergency call 112. Never approach a fallen conductor.
